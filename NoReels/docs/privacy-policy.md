# NoReels — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 17 October 2026_

NoReels helps you limit short-video feeds (YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels,
Snapchat Spotlight, TikTok) on your Android phone.

## What the app accesses
- **Accessibility service** — used only to see which app and screen are currently open, so
  NoReels can detect short-video feeds, count the time you spend in them, and show a block screen
  when your limit is reached. NoReels does not read your messages, passwords, or what you type.
- **Usage access (optional)** — used only to show how long each app was open today.
- **Notifications** — used to show your session countdown, a 1-minute warning, and an alert if
  blocking is turned off.

## What the app stores
Your limits, daily usage totals, session history, and a small log of unclear detections.
All of it is stored **only on your device**.

## What the app shares
Nothing. NoReels has no account, no server, no analytics, and no ads. No data leaves your device
and nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

## Deleting your data
Uninstall the app, or go to Settings → Apps → NoReels → Storage → Clear data.

## Children
NoReels does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
